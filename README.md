# CNG Fleet Conversion - Impact Quantification Report (Sample)

Twelve-month verified impact assessment of a 40-vehicle compressed natural gas
conversion programme, prepared by CircularEnergies as a sample deliverable.

## Contents

- **`CE_Sample_IQR_CNG_Fleet_Conversion.docx`** - The published Impact Quantification
  Report. Emissions accounting, financial performance, DFI indicator alignment, and
  eligibility assessment against CDM AMS-III.S.

- **`CE_IQR_CNG_Fleet_RawData.xlsx`** - Complete raw data behind every figure in the
  report. Every input, every emission factor, and the full baseline-to-net-avoided
  calculation chain. Eight tabs, reconciled to the report totals.

## What this demonstrates

- GHG Protocol Scope 1 mobile combustion accounting with methane slip properly deducted
- Segment-level modelling of bi-fuel and dual-fuel conversions with different fuels
  and substitution rates
- Reconciliation from telematics and dispensing records to reported tCO₂e and ₦
- DFI indicator population for AfDB, IFC-CAFI, FCDO, PCNGi and Nigeria NDC frameworks
- Sensitivity analysis on the highest-value refinement available to the programme

## Disclaimer

Meridian Logistics Nigeria Ltd is a fictional entity. All figures are simulated for
demonstration purposes and must not be relied upon for investment, disclosure or
compliance decisions.
